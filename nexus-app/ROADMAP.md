## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **AI Costs**: Implement caching and rate limiting
- **Scalability**: Use serverless architecture
- **Data Privacy**: Implement strong encryption

### Business Risks
- **Competition**: Focus on superior AI and UX
- **User Acquisition**: Invest in content marketing
- **Retention**: Build sticky features
